Connect your Firebase project

This app stores your cards in your own free Firebase project so they sync live between your iPad and other devices. Open firebase-config.js and paste in your project's web config. The README lists each step.

Open your deck

Pick a username and password. The first time, this creates your deck. After that, enter the same username and password on any device to open it.

Add from your notes

Type or paste your notes in either format below, then tap Import. A # heading sets the topic for the cards under it, and answers can run over several lines.
